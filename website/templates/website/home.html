Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between cold pressed and refined oil?

Cold pressed oil is extracted by crushing the seed slowly in a wooden chekku at under 40°C, with no chemicals involved. Refined oil is extracted with heat and solvents such as hexane, then bleached and deodorised. Cold pressed oil keeps the natural nutrients, colour and aroma of the seed; refined oil does not.

How long does cold pressed oil stay good?

Six months from the pressing date when stored in a cool, dark place with the cap closed tight. Keep it away from the stove and from direct sunlight. A little sediment at the bottom is normal — it is seed matter, not spoilage.

Is the sediment at the bottom of the bottle a problem?

No. We filter by natural settling instead of chemical clarification, so fine seed particles remain. It is a sign the oil has not been over-processed.

Which oil should I use for daily cooking?

For everyday South Indian cooking, groundnut and sesame (gingelly) oil handle regular frying temperatures well. Coconut oil suits poriyal, curries and hair care. Mustard oil works for pickles and North Indian dishes. Many families keep two or three and rotate them.
